FindMe

A way to meet the people you pass in ordinary life: dating, friendship, and small groups.

FindMe is a meeting app. It is for dating, for friendship, and for groups. The people on it are the ones you might already walk past.

The product gives that meeting a place to start, instead of leaving it as a face you remember and cannot find again.
