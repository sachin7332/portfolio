Measuremint

A private place for information you would not put in a message, shared only with the people you choose.

Measuremint keeps personal, confidential information in the app and lets someone share a piece of it with another person, with that exchange kept tight.

The product exists so the information is still reachable later, and still not sitting in an open chat.
