Earlier work  ·  Mobile app

Waves

A social app for sharing where you are, so the people you care about can find you and join.

Waves is a mobile social app. Someone shares where they are. Friends can join them, plan an event, send an invitation, or simply see what people nearby are doing.

The product is about presence: a small signal that turns into a plan, rather than a feed you scroll and forget.
